Which material?

The right choice depends on what the part will live with. Heat, water, sunlight and flexing all matter.

MaterialGood forWatch out for
PLAGifts, signs, name tags and display pieces. Crisp detail and the widest colour choice.Softens in high heat, such as a hot car or direct sun behind glass.
PETGWorkshop parts, brackets and anything that needs to be tougher or see damp and daylight.Slightly less crisp fine detail than PLA.
TPUFlexible parts such as grips, bumpers, feet and seals.Too soft for rigid, load-bearing parts.

Not sure? Tell me where the part will be used and I will recommend one.

How it works

    Size, colour, what it is for, plus any photos, sketches or files.

  2. Get a quote

    I check it can be printed, suggest a material and confirm price and timing.

Questions

What should I send?

Whatever you have: an STL file, a sketch, a photo of the part, or a short description with measurements. Photos with a ruler or coin next to the part help a lot.

Can you design something from scratch?

Yes. Send a sketch, a photo of the part or the measurements and I will model it. Any design time is included in the quote so there are no surprises.

How much does it cost?

It depends on size, material, print time and any design work. I give you a price before I print anything.

Can I collect, or do you post?

Either. We agree which when I confirm the quote.

Can you match a specific colour?

Tell me the colour you want and I will say what is available.
