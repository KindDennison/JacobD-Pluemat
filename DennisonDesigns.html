Made to measure

Custom helmet sizing

Helmets are printed to your measurement rather than a generic size, so they sit properly instead of being close enough. Set the slider to your head diameter and I will scale the model to match before printing.

  • Printed in sections and assembled, then finished by hand.
  • Children's sizes available, measured the same way.
  • Bring me a measurement or a tape measure, either works.
  • Lining and strap fittings can be added on request.

Adult S

Adult small. Approx. head circumference 22″.

Not sure of your diameter? Measure around the widest part of the head, just above the ears, then divide by 3.14. For children, measure them standing still with the tape level all the way round.

Materials

Three filaments cover almost everything I get asked for. If you are unsure, tell me how the part will be used and I will pick.

Filament comparison
Material Best for Notes
PLA Names, tags, display pieces, anything decorative Widest colour range and the crispest detail. Not suited to heat or strong sunlight.
PETG Brackets, adapters, mounts, outdoor parts Tougher and more heat resistant than PLA with a slight flex. Fewer colour options.
TPU Seals, feet, gaskets, anything that needs to bend Flexible rubber-like finish. Prints slower, so larger parts take longer.

How it works

Four steps, and usually only one of them involves you doing anything.

  1. Send the idea

    An STL, a sketch, a photo, or just a description. Measurements help, and a ruler or coin in a photo is enough to scale from.

  2. Get a quote

    A price and a rough turnaround. Design time is included, so there is no separate charge for working the model out.

  3. I design and print

    I build the model, print it, clean it up and check it fits or works before it goes anywhere.

  4. Collect or posted

    Collect in person or post out, whichever suits. I will send a photo before it leaves either way.

Questions

The ones that come up most often. Anything else, just ask.

What do you need from me to get started?

Anything you have. An STL, a sketch on paper, a photo of the part with a ruler or coin in shot for scale, or just written measurements. If it is a replacement part, a photo of where it fits is often the most useful thing.

Can you design something from scratch?

Yes. Design work is included in the quote, so you are not paying separately for the modelling. If it is a big job I will confirm the design direction before printing anything.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size, the material, the print time and how much design work is needed. Send the details and you will get a fixed price before anything is made.

Do you post, or is it collection only?

Either. Collection in person is fine, and posting is not a problem for anything that fits in a parcel box. Larger items may need a courier quote.

Can you match a colour?

Usually. I keep a range of colours in stock and can order specific ones in. Send a photo or a colour reference and I will tell you how close I can get.
